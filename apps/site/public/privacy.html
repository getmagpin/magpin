Privacy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Magpin has no server, no account and no tracking. Nothing you do with Magpin is sent to us: there is no “us” to send it to. The Chrome extension and the Figma plugin work on your computer, and only talk to the sites your boards come from.

The Chrome extension

  • It reads a board only when you click Collect this board, on the board page you have open (Pinterest, Are.na, Cosmos or Savee). It collects the addresses of the board's images, its title and its link.
  • For an Are.na channel, it asks Are.na's public API (api.are.na) for the channel's images.
  • Boards you add to your moodboard are kept in Chrome's storage on your computer (chrome.storage.local), until you remove them or clear the moodboard.
  • Copy for Figma puts the board's image addresses on your clipboard. Download ZIP downloads the images from the source site's image servers and saves a file on your computer.
  • It sends no analytics, uses no cookies of its own and runs no code from elsewhere.

The Figma plugin

  • It reads what you paste into it (a board copied with the extension) or the ZIP and images you import.
  • It downloads the images straight from their source's image servers (Pinterest, Are.na, Cosmos, Savee) to place them in your Figma file. Once placed, they are part of your file and stored by Figma, under Figma's own privacy policy.
  • Your settings (layout, columns, spacing, palette) are kept on your computer with Figma's plugin storage.
  • It sends no analytics and makes no other request.

This website

A static page: no cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts. Its fonts are served from the same place as the page.
